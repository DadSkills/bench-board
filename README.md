# Bench Board

A hockey coaching app in a single file. Open `index.html` in any browser.

- **Board**: rink whiteboard with X's, O's, pucks, player numbers and drawing in red, black, blue or white. Full ice or half ice, rotatable.
- **Roster**: players with number, name and position, split into two teams, with highlight colours.
- **Lines** and **Shifts**: forward lines, defence pairs and a shift and ice-time tracker for full-ice games.
- **U9**: half-ice 4-on-4 with two teams in pairs, including shift and ice-time tracking.

Everything saves in the browser on the device you use.
